# Credits and licensing

## v2 materials: shipped derivatives of CC0 PBR textures

Individual asset pages were fetched and checked for CC0 before downloading.
Exact downloaded URLs and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in `data/assets.lock.json`.
Raw files, metadata and captured asset pages are cached under the gitignored
`data/asset_cache/<ID>/`; `scripts/build.py assets` re-downloads missing files
and rejects changed bytes rather than silently updating the lock.

repair shapes, tyre wear, cracks exposed by blending, worn markings and terrain
blend masks are generated by this project. No other track or paid-mod content
is incorporated. Foliage images/geometry and water remain v1 procedural assets.
