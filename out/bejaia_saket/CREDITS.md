# Credits and licensing

Phase 4c roadside retaining blocks, fences, generic signs and bus shelters are
original procedural geometry. The sign atlas (chevrons, 50 km/h and generic bus
pictogram) is generated by `scripts/roadside.py`, with no logos or copied imagery;
these original assets are dedicated under CC0-1.0. Existing credited rock, bark,
metal and plaster textures are reused. Branched tree variants and two-octave
terrain masks are procedural derivatives of the existing sources.
Poly Haven **pine_tree_01** photographic twig diffuse/alpha is now used directly
in the pine cards: https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01, **CC0-1.0** under
https://polyhaven.com/license. Source: `pine_tree_01_twig_diff_1k.png`; exact CDN
URL and SHA-256 are pinned in `data/foliage_assets.lock.json`. Only the top-left
twig island is cropped/resampled with alpha edge filtering; no model geometry
is imported. All other leaf silhouettes remain original procedural art.
No third-party model pack is integrated in this phase.
The focused art pass adds original procedural stained-concrete and soil/needle-litter atlases and
ground-conforming patches, also dedicated under CC0-1.0; no new art downloads.

Phase 4a landscape and distant foothills reuse the existing Copernicus GLO-30
DEM tiles; the expanded shoreline is derived from the cached OpenStreetMap
Algeria extract (attributions below). Noise detail is procedural. Landscape
grass/rock and expanded sea reuse existing credited materials and textures;
no new third-party art assets were downloaded for this phase.

## Phase 4b procedural vegetation

`scripts/foliage.py` generates all new foliage pixels and plant geometry from
deterministic procedural shapes/noise: Aleppo pine, stone pine, olive, cypress,
maquis, reeds and dry grass, three shape variants each. Six 1K RGBA atlases cover
canopies and whole-plant impostors with green/dark/silver tints. These original
procedural assets are dedicated to **CC0-1.0**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No external foliage images,
other tracks, paid mods or Poly Haven model geometry are used. Existing bark
textures retain the CC0 attribution in the material asset inventory above.
Placement is an artistic deterministic approximation, not surveyed tree data.
Default tree assets are external procedural TreeFX BIN archives, emitted using
the documented wire contract read from Content Manager's open-source converter
(https://github.com/gro-ove/actools, Ms-PL); the source is cited field-by-field in
`docs/TREEFX_FORMAT.md`. No CM software or third-party models are redistributed.
GrassFX uses the same original procedural atlas for low maquis/dry tufts.
