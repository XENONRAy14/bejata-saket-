# RN24 summit approach → Saket

## Boundary and distance convention

The canonical graded road has 24,086.997 m of projected chainage and 24,114.408 m
of 3D centerline distance. Prominence-based analysis identifies the 323.239 m
main summit at old projected km 9.591581 / 3D km 9.602888. The subsequent main
descent drops 269.720 m over 5,633.720 m before the next major trough. This is
not the later secondary 102 m or 42 m summit. The unsmoothed DEM maximum is
112 m farther along the same broad summit; **the actual graded driving road**
determines where climbing ends.

Pole/grid slot 0, pit slot 0 (Practice), and hotlap spawn share the exact point
1,000.000 m before the graded summit along the 3D centerline:

- old 3D chainage: 8,602.888437 m;
- WGS84: 36.7750432497 N, 5.0265559902 E;
- road elevation: 283.951 m; direction: toward Saket (roughly north);
- net climb to summit: 39.288 m; a small dip remains in this kilometre;
- remaining road from pole spawn: 15,511.520 m, not all continuously downhill.

The physical road begins 30 m behind the pole spawn, at old 3D chainage
8,572.888437 m. A visible `1WALL_START_END` end barrier closes that rear pad.
There is no old climb road, city decor or old TreeFX placement hidden behind
the cut. The source route and original full arrays remain as rebuild inputs,
not as meshes in the KN5. Distant terrain may still show the real surrounding
hills: removing the old road does not mean deleting the regional landscape.

## Grid, start, finish and cameras

Four grid and four pit slots are spaced 8 m apart behind the pole, with mild
lateral staggering, all facing forward along the curved road. All modes use
the exact 1 km point for slot 0. Other slots necessarily start farther back.
The start timing gate and the visual transverse paint line are **3 m ahead
of the pole**, so a stopped vehicle can cross the gate. The summit is therefore
997 m after the timing line, while it remains exactly 1,000 m from the pole.
The finish gate retains 50 m of runoff before the Saket road endpoint. Two
internal timing gates divide the retained timed length into thirds.

The fixed curved-start layout bypasses the old 120 m straight-staging search,
which otherwise moves the spawn almost to the summit. Existing Blender road,
start/summit and finish-check cameras are regenerated. There was no replay
`cameras.ini` or recorded AI line in the old package; none is fabricated here.
AI races remain unsupported until real lines are recorded.

UI length includes the 30 m pad (15,542 m rounded). Map and outline cover the
active road only; UI geotags identify the new pole spawn. New kilometre markers
count from the new physical road boundary, not the old city start or official
RN24 kilometre posts. Previously town-specific buildings, signs, shelters and
species zones retain their canonical geographic ranges and are dropped when
outside the new layout rather than being shifted onto the mountain.

## Reproduction and verification

```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/build.py all
.venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s scripts -p 'test_[!e]*.py'
~/tools/blender-3.6.23-linux-x64/blender -b --python-exit-code 1 -P scripts/test_exporter.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/validate.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/validate_shortened.py
```

The exporter smoke test requires Blender's `bpy`, so it runs separately from
ordinary Python tests. `scripts/shortening.py` cuts only after full-route
smoothing/grading. Existing samples and ribbon cross-sections are copied
exactly; only the cut/spawn/gate cross-sections are interpolated. No retained
road is resmoothed, regraded or translated. A new mesh/chunk layout is exported.
The driving surface coefficients in `surfaces.ini` do not change.

The Blender scene uses relative texture paths. Keep `blend/` and `textures/`
beside each other when moving the project to Windows; generated `textures/v2/`
is included in the delivered source archive and regenerated by the build.
TreeFX instances/models remain external to the main Blender scene/KN5. The
two `out/preview/shortened/after/` views are Cycles previews, not AC captures.

Machine-readable measurements: `data/shortening_info.json`. Canonical full
arrays: `data/full_centerline.npz`; shortened arrays: `data/centerline.npz`.
`scripts/test_shortening.py` covers summit choice, exact 3D distance, retained
suffix identity, grid direction/clearance, gate order and invalid inputs.
The full build regenerates road/terrain, collisions, decor, TreeFX placements,
used model archives, arrows/materials, CSP configuration, UI/map and package.

## Human AC test still required

1. Back up your installed track, then replace its folder with the complete new
   build. Do not overlay it on an old install or install LFS pointer files.
2. Launch Practice in slot 0: road climbs toward Saket; the summit is 1 km ahead
   with a small real dip en route, then the main descent begins immediately.
3. Check rear end barrier collision at low speed, grid spacing/direction, no
   floating cars and no old city climb behind the pad.
4. Confirm timing starts when crossing the transverse line, both internal
   gates work, and finish timing completes before the 50 m runoff.
5. Verify mini-map position/direction and that it contains only the short route.
6. Inspect TreeFX/GrassFX, horizon, retained roadside geometry, FPS, Pure shadows
   and RainFX. Static tests do not prove native rendering, wet grip or collisions.

The separate professional-assets 300 m pilot is blocked until the user's local
`F:\route bejaia Saket assetto corssa` folder is accessible. No claim is made
that those local packs have been inspected, licensed, imported or optimized.
